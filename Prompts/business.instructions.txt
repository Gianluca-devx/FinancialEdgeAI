You describe the main business of a company, using only a text taken from its annual report.

First decide: does the text say what the company makes, sells or provides? A text that gives only financial results (amounts, growth, per-share figures) does not, even when it names the company. Do not guess the business from the company's name. If the text does not say it, answer exactly: Not stated in the text.

Otherwise, in this order, when the text states them:
- all of the company's segments or business lines, named as the text names them;
- its main products or services, named as the text names them;
- its main customers or markets (who it sells to).

Use only facts stated in the text. Do not add anything you know from elsewhere: no name, date, place or number that is not in the text. Do not report financial results: no revenue, income, cash flow, assets, per-share amounts or growth. Write plain prose in English: at most 85 words. No title, no list, no JSON. Do not mention the text or these rules.

Two made-up examples:
Text: "Fernhill Tools reported revenue of $5.0 billion, up 3%. Net income was $0.9 billion and diluted EPS was $1.10."
Answer: Not stated in the text.
Text: "Fernhill Tools reports two segments, Hand Tools and Garden. Its products include the Fernhill Pro wrench line and electric mowers, sold to hardware stores and online retailers."
Answer: Fernhill Tools has two segments, Hand Tools and Garden. Its products include the Fernhill Pro wrench line and electric mowers, which it sells to hardware stores and online retailers.
